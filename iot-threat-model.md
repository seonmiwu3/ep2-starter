# IoT Threat Model: Robot Vacuum

**Asset:** Family life privacy—specifically the home floorplan map, cleaning schedules (which reveal when the house is empty), and any live camera or sensor feeds inside living spaces. 

**Threat:** A remote attacker or unauthorized snooper trying to peep into the home by guessing or reusing weak cloud-account passwords, or exploiting unpatched firmware over the network. 

**Response:** Use a strong unique password with two-factor authentication (2FA) on the companion app, keep automatic firmware updates enabled, disable live camera streaming or cover the camera when not needed, and place the vacuum on an isolated guest Wi-Fi network so it cannot access personal family computers or phones.
